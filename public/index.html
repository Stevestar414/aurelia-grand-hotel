OUR STORY

A Century of Quiet Discretion

From a 1928 grand European salon to a contemporary landmark.

Hospitality is the art of noticing before being asked.

Aurelia Grand opened in 1928 with a singular philosophy: a guest should never have to ask twice. Our concierge team learns your preferred morning espresso by day two and has your driver waiting before your flight lands.

Following a four-year architectural restoration, the property pairs original limestone facades and herringbone parquet with whisper-quiet acoustic engineering and geothermal climate control.
